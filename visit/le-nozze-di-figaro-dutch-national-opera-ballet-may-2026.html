Le nozze di Figaro

Reference ↗
Opera·

Dutch National Opera & Ballet, Amsterdam

Conflicted

Serebrennikov clearly had a lot to say. The first half was genuinely cool... ringtones played on the harpsichord mad me laugh sooo much, a man on all fours (all grotesque, but the point!), Susanna playing everyone like a fiddle (it should really be called Le nozze di Susanna). Then came the art gallery and its fake massacre. And the mirrors. I checked out.

Kulchynska's Countess was gorgeous though -- two arias that made all the mirrors and getting blasted with light in my eyes almost worth it.
