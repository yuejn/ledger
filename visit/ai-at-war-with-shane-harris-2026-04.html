AI at War — with Shane Harris

Talk·

De Balie, Amsterdam

Washington Post journalist Shane Harris in conversation with Yoeri Albrecht on the use of artificial intelligence in modern warfare, surveillance, and intelligence operations.

Enriched

Journalist Shane Harris talked about AI, mass surveillance, and cyber warfare, anchored around the Anthropic-Pentagon standoff as a case study in how entangled tech companies and intelligence agencies have become.

I left feeling oddly comforted. Not because the picture he painted was reassuring (it was DEPRESSING AS FUCK) but because a packed room of people clearly shared the same unease and discomfort. It helps to know the worry is collective. It helps me, at least.

My friends were less enthused. There was no call to action at the end of the talk. There was "a lot of talking". They wanted technical depth and found the "black box" framing too wooey. But for me, after being bombarded with the tech industry's impression of AI, the geopolitical and civil liberties angle was exactly the lens I needed to hear.
