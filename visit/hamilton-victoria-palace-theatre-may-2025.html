Hamilton

More about this work ↗
Musical·

Victoria Palace Theatre, London

Hamilton, the hit musical. Book, Music and Lyrics by Lin-Manuel Miranda. Choreography by Andy Blankenbuehler. Directed by Thomas Kail.

Loved itMoved

I went into this completely blind. Knew nothing about it. Nada. I did know that it was controversial; and that some more enlightened folk I knew did not like it -- but ... I'm sorry, I fuckin' loved it. It took me a while to get into it (the first twenty seconds was me going: ARE THERE ANY SURTITLES?) but then I was HOOKED.

I liked the flow, the pacing, the characters, the staging, and goddamn -- the music and lyricism is excellent. Hamilton was my most played album that year.

"If you stand for nothing, what'll you fall for?" remains a GOATed line.
