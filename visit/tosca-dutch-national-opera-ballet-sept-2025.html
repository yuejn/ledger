Tosca

Opera·

Dutch National Opera & Ballet, Amsterdam

Love, power and betrayal take centre stage in Tosca, a psychological thriller by Giacomo Puccini. Barrie Kosky’s razor-sharp production, which received high praise in 2022, returns to Dutch National Opera. When singer Floria Tosca’s lover is imprisoned by the sadistic chief of police Scarpia, she becomes entangled in a dangerous game of power. With its stark contrasts and sharp character portrayals, Tosca is one of Puccini’s most suspenseful operas.
