JUN 2024

Algorithm ocean true blood moves

Dance·

Studio Boekman, Amsterdam

After its successful world premiere at Performa Biennial 2023 in New York, Hartwig Art Foundation will present Julien Creuzet’s Algorithm ocean true blood moves in Amsterdam. Creuzet, who represents France at the 60th Venice Biennale, the first French-Caribbean artist to do so, will stage his largest performance piece to date at Studio Boekman.
