Harry Styles — Together, Together

w/ Robyn

Gig·

Johan Cruijff ArenA, Amsterdam

JoyfulConflicted

Went with the GIRLSSSS! And it was so cute and fun! My "huh" comes in at knowing I spent roughly the same amount for Taylor and Beyoncé and they had a million costume and set changes and dancing; and Harry took his jacket off and did a little dance for one song. I love Harry! He's from my side of the country. But I just could not stop thinking about that double standard once I noticed it. :-) VERY FUN THOUGH. Would go again.

Loved, loved, loved seeing Robyn (finally!). Totally dreamy.
