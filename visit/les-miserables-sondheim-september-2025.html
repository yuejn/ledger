Les Misérables

Musical·

Sondheim Theatre, London

JoyfulDazzled

Came out emotionally wrung out in the best way. An absolute rollercoaster. It's long, it's sprawling, there's a lot happening, and that's like the point. The sheer ambition of cramming so much story into a matinee is its own kind of miracle. Left me genuinely in awe of what the musical as a format can pull off. I fuckin' love the theatre.
