Les Misérables

Musical·

Sondheim Theatre, London

Set in 19th-century France, Les Misérables is an epic tale of redemption and revolution. It follows ex-convict Jean Valjean who, after breaking parole, dedicates his life to raising an orphan named Cosette. While he seeks absolution, he is ruthlessly hunted by the uncompromising police inspector Javert across decades.

JoyfulDazzled

Went in completely blind — no book, no film, no music — and came out emotionally wrung out in the best way. An absolute rollercoaster. It's long, it's sprawling, there's a lot happening, and that's like the point. The sheer ambition of cramming so much story into a matinee is its own kind of miracle. Left me genuinely in awe of what the musical as a format can pull off. I fuckin' love the theatre.
