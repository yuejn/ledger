MAR 2024

Oedipus Rex / Antigone

Opera·

Dutch National Opera & Ballet, Amsterdam

Two classical tragedies Ambitious works for chorus, vocal soloists and dancers This double bill — Stravinsky’s Oedipus Rex (1927) and Samy Moussa’s Antigone (2020) — centres on the tragic collision between the political and the personal. In this new production, stage director Mart van Berckel and director-choreographer Nanine Linning bring together the worlds of classical tragedy and of twentieth- and twenty-first-century music in a unique theatrical experience.
