The Knife of Dawn

Opera·

Studio Boekman, Amsterdam

·Opera Forward Festival 2026

The Knife of Dawn tells the compelling story of the Guyanese political activist and poet Martin Carter, who was imprisoned without charge for his fight for his country’s autonomy.

ImpressedMoved

Every time I walk into Studio Boekman I think about the time I asked about tax benefits on their seat donation scheme and never heard back — I'd be halfway there by now! Anyway. Mkosana was an absolute marvel — carried the whole thing on his shoulders, which felt entirely right given the material. The staging was minimal but landed with real impact. Excellent night.
