The Mousetrap

Play·

St Martin's Theatre, London

Agatha Christie's record-breaking murder mystery, the longest-running play in the world.

Solid

As one of my stock quiz questions, longest running West End show, let's go!

The Mousetrap is one of Agatha Christie's famous murder mysteries -- a "whodunit" set on a very stormy day in Monkswell Manor. All the action takes place in the living room, and I really liked the set -- with lots of doors and stairs and a window that opened to the wind and snow. I would be very curious to see how the set design has changed over the past several decades!

I do not know at all how to write about this without spoiling anything so I would say: I thought the play was solid. It was a great third, or fourth, round of my West Ending.

And Stefan Chanyaem was incredible -- both comedic and effectively creepy. He stole the show.
