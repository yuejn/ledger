Romeo en Julia

Ballet·

Dutch National Opera & Ballet, Amsterdam

Loved it

I watched this with a bunch of people who had never been to the theatre before -- really, there was a whole classroom of kids who kept talking and clapping and it all came to a head when, after intermission, the curtains draw to Romeo and Juliet in bed together and one of the teenagers loudly went, "EW!" That made me laugh.
