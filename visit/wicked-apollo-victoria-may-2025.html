Wicked

Musical·

Apollo Victoria Theatre, London

The untold story of the witches of Oz: the unlikely friendship between the green-skinned, brilliant Elphaba and the ambitious, popular Glinda — and how one became the Wicked Witch and the other Glinda the Good. Based on Gregory Maguire's novel, it reimagines The Wizard of Oz as a tale of political manipulation and personal courage.

Joyful

Went in completely blind again — no soundtrack, no film, nothing — and came out slightly stunned. Absolutely bonkers in the best way. We were in the nosebleeds again, which at this point is becoming a theme, and honestly it was a stretch too far. Michael remained characteristically neutral throughout.
